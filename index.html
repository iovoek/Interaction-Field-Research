Exchange Is the Equation

A structural insight about complex systems: the direction from which you analyze them determines whether they thrive or degrade. One framework. Nine domains. An ongoing investigation.

By Glen Brackmann with AI research assistance

In most complex systems, we start from what is easy to measure and try to work backward toward what actually matters. Stock prices instead of trading activity. Engagement metrics instead of genuine connection. Molecular targets instead of living cell behavior.

This seems intuitive. But in complex systems, it actively degrades the thing it is trying to measure. The errors compound. And the more complex the system, the worse it gets.

This project explores the alternative: start from the deep structure, the actual pattern of genuine interactions, and let surface metrics be outputs rather than inputs. Researchers across many fields have been saying versions of this for decades. What none of them have done is connect these observations into a single formal framework.

That is what we are building here.
