Citations & Sources

Every claim in this research is grounded in published data, peer-reviewed literature, or verifiable public records. Foundational Theory & Mathematics

KYLE-1985
Continuous Auctions and Insider Trading
Kyle, Albert S.
Econometrica, 53(6), 1315–1335, 1985.
The foundational market microstructure model from which the price uncertainty function σp = σv / √n is derived. The mathematical form matching the pyramid error function σapex = σ0 · H / √N is the structural correspondence at the core of the Orientation Theorem.
JSTOR
SHANNON-1948
A Mathematical Theory of Communication
Shannon, Claude E.
Bell System Technical Journal, 27(3), 379–423, 1948.
The entropy proof that zero transactions implies maximum price entropy implies no value is derived from Shannon entropy. Used to establish that transaction volume constitutes value rather than merely correlating with it.
PDF
ARTHUR-1994
Increasing Returns and Path Dependence in the Economy
Arthur, W. Brian.
University of Michigan Press, 1994.
The foundational work on increasing returns and path dependence in complex economic systems. Provides theoretical grounding for why interaction fields exhibit threshold behavior and why early orientation choices compound over time.
Publisher
METCALFE-2013
Metcalfe's Law after 40 Years of Ethernet
Metcalfe, Robert.
IEEE Computer, 46(12), 26–31, 2013.
Network value scaling with n² connections. The Interaction Field Equation modifies this by distinguishing genuine from surface-level connections, explaining why platforms with the same user count can have vastly different value densities.
IEEE
CURRIER-2019
70 Percent of Value in Tech is Driven by Network Effects
Currier, James.
NFX, 2019.
Industry analysis establishing that network effects account for the majority of defensible value in technology companies. Provides empirical context for the Interaction Field Equation's application to platform businesses.
NFX
SOROS-2013
Fallibility, Reflexivity, and the Human Uncertainty Principle
Soros, George.
Journal of Economic Methodology, 20(4), 309–329, 2013.
Reflexivity in financial markets: participant beliefs alter the fundamentals they are trying to measure. Provides theoretical grounding for why surface-metric optimization creates self-reinforcing distortions.
Taylor & Francis
KRAUSS-2017
Statistical Arbitrage Pairs Trading Strategies: Review and Outlook
Krauss, Christopher.
Journal of Economic Surveys, 31(2), 513–545, 2017.
Review of quantitative trading strategies and their limitations. Provides context for the trading card market analysis that originated this research project.
Wiley
GASSER-2022
Value and Price Formation of Collectibles in the Tension between Community and Marketization
Gasser, Jakob.
Max Planck Institute Working Paper, 2022.
Academic analysis of how collectible markets derive value from community interaction rather than intrinsic properties. Directly supports the trading card market case study that originated this research.

Urban Planning & Community Systems

JACOBS-1961
The Death and Life of Great American Cities
Jacobs, Jane.
Random House, 1961.
The most direct independent discovery of the Orientation Theorem in urban planning. Jacobs argued that planners were analyzing cities from the wrong direction: optimizing for surface metrics (traffic flow, density ratios, zoning categories) while destroying the deep structure (mixed use, short blocks, aged buildings, concentration of people) that actually generates urban vitality. Her work is a direct precursor to the Backwards Problem concept.
Publisher
PUTNAM-2000
Bowling Alone: The Collapse and Revival of American Community
Putnam, Robert D.
Simon and Schuster, 2000.
Longitudinal study of social capital decline in American communities. Provides empirical grounding for the claim that surface-metric optimization in urban planning destroys the interaction field that generates community value.
Publisher

LEE-2004
The Triple-A Supply Chain
Lee, Hau L.
Harvard Business Review, 82(10), 102–112, 2004.
The Agility, Adaptability, Alignment framework for supply chain resilience. Lee's emphasis on alignment (shared goals and information across the supply chain) is the supply chain equivalent of the Interaction Field: value emerges from the quality of connections between nodes, not from optimizing individual node efficiency.
HBR
SHEFFI-2005
The Resilient Enterprise: Overcoming Vulnerability for Competitive Advantage
Sheffi, Yossi.
MIT Press, 2005.
Analysis of supply chain resilience and the role of redundancy, flexibility, and collaboration. Supports the argument that supply chain optimization for surface metrics (cost, lead time) at the expense of deep structural health (trust, information flow) produces fragile systems.
MIT Press

The Zone of Proximal Development and the social construction of knowledge. Origin & Inspiration

CHOI-PYRAMID
Top-Down Pyramid Construction Theory
Choi, Huni.
Independent researcher. Instagram: @hunichoipyramid. YouTube presentation, 2025–2026.
Independent researcher proposing that the Egyptian pyramids were constructed from the top down rather than the bottom up, based on keying stone analysis and construction logistics. This theory was the direct origin of the Backwards Problem concept: the insight that a finished structure can be completely misread if you assume the direction in which it was built.
Instagram YouTube
DAMI-LEE-2026
Pyramid Construction Video (January 2026)
Dami Lee Arch (YouTube channel).
YouTube, January 2026. Approximately 10 million views.
Architecture and design YouTube channel that presented Huni Choi's pyramid construction theory to a broad audience. Watching this video was the direct inspiration for this research project. The visual demonstration of how a structure built top-down looks identical to one built bottom-up, but has completely different structural logic, crystallized the Backwards Problem concept.
YouTube Channel
HISTORY-GRANITE
Egyptian Pyramid Research
History for GRANITE (YouTube channel).
YouTube. 331K subscribers.
YouTube channel presenting new evidence and analysis on Egyptian pyramid construction, challenging conventional Egyptology with detailed archaeological examination. Provided key research context for the pyramid construction arguments in this work.
YouTube

Simulation & Reproducibility

SIM-V5
Social Media Algorithm Simulation v5 (Fully Empirically Anchored)
Brackmann, Glen, with AI research assistance.
GitHub: iovoek/Interaction-Field-Research, simulation/social_media_algorithm_sim_v5.py, 2026.
